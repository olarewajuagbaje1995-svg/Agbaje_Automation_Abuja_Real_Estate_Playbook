1 · What goes wrong

When enquiries grow faster than your admin, the gaps start to cost you.

A busy agency in Abuja can receive enquiries from a website, phone calls, WhatsApp messages and agents all in one day. Without one consistent process, good opportunities sit in different phones and notebooks.

An honest note. Automation improves consistency and visibility. It does not replace professional judgement, good management or a person following up with a customer. It works best when your team agrees on the process first.
